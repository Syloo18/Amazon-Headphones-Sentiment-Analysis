# Amazon-Earphones-Sentiment-Analysis (RStudio)

## Overview
A comprehensive analysis of reviewers' sentiment values of Earphones purchased on Amazon

## Objectives
The objective is to unearth the latent topics of interest and sentiments of the customers' reviews
To identify & discuss actionable insights to guide business decisions for Amazon

## Dataset
Kat, S. (2019) Amazon Earphones Reviews dataset features a sample size of 14,338 reviews of 10 different earphones on Amazon
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shitalkat/amazonearphonesreviews

## Results
Of the 13 Topics identified, the top 4 topics by sentiment scores are 'Excellent Performance', 'Worth the Money', 'Bass Quality' & 'Good Performance'. Conversely, the bottom 4 topics are 'Regret Purchase', 'Bluetooth connectivity', 'After-sales Service' & 'Bad Performance'.
